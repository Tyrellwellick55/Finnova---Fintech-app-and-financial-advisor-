Privacy Policy

Data we collect

Identity, authentication, preferences, and onboarding selections power the shell flow. Financial data including income, expenses, budgets, and goals are used to render the dashboard and connected modules. We collect the minimum product data required to provide the service.

How we use it

Your data is used exclusively to power Finnova features — analytics, autopilot recommendations, and reporting. We never sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for purposes outside the product.

Your rights

You can export all your data at any time from Finance Settings. You can request account deletion by contacting support. Local development instances should treat all sample data as non-production.
